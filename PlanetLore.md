# Alpha Kerbonis System

### Alpha Kerbonis 

Alpha Kerbonis is a dim, red dwarf star. It is 4.2 billion years old, a bit younger than Kerbol. It is a small - medium sized red dwarf, so its lifespan 
is estimated to be around 6-8 trillion years. For now, Alpha Kerbonis is in a state of low activity, so not many solar flares and radiation.

### Oasis

Oasis is a very hot planet with life. Its life mainly consists of fungi. You can find small mushrooms dotted across its surface, near beaches, or in the basins of craters.
Oasis has a large ocean with the temperature of 140-150 degrees Fahrenheit, or 60 degrees Celsius. Among all the bodies in the Alpha Kerbonis system, oasis has the most beaches.
This is because of its mainly flat surface and abundance of craters. You can find tons of cozy crater beaches near sea level.
